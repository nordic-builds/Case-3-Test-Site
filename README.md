<h1 id="interviewer-guide">Interviewer Guide</h1>
<p>This page is for the interviewers only. It holds the script, the
consent form, the questionnaires and the interview questions for each
session. <strong>Do not share it with participants</strong>: the
debriefing explains the real purpose of the study.</p>
<h2 id="session-at-a-glance">Session at a glance</h2>
<p>The session takes about 33 minutes on Microsoft Teams. One of us is
the moderator and speaks to the participant, and one is the observer and
takes notes without intervening.</p>

<h2 id="before-the-session">Before the session</h2>
<p>The participant receives an email in advance. It must contain:</p>
<ul>
<li>The date, time and Teams meeting link, and the guide for sharing the
screen in Teams.</li>
<li>Their participant ID.</li>
<li>The link to the <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeok2nbN-E6R2zKpRNv0_kGEClY4ZbMz7Vtx2fFvwpjUFEtdQ/viewform?usp=dialog">GDPR
form</a> (information, gender, age group and written consent), to be
completed before the session.</li>
<li>Link 1 (<a href="https://forms.gle/wfFPgBoJKJLGPKWj8">UES-SF</a>)
and link 2 (<a href="https://forms.gle/AoGbdH4jq9maggZE7">EMCB</a>),
with the line: &quot;Please do not open them yet, only when we ask you to
during the meeting.&quot;</li>
<li>A note to join from their own computer with a microphone, from a
quiet place.</li>
</ul>
<p>Moderator: have the prototype link of the participant&#39;s condition (A
to D) ready to send in the Teams chat.</p>

<h2 id="script">Script</h2>
<h3 id="1-welcome-2-min">1. Welcome (2 min)</h3>
<blockquote>
<p>&quot;Hi, and thank you for your time. I&#39;m [name], and this is [name], who
will be taking notes. We&#39;re Information Science students at Aalborg
University Copenhagen, and this is part of our student project.</p>
<p>Today we&#39;re testing the search function of an online shop prototype
that we built ourselves. It isn&#39;t connected to any real shop, and no
real purchases are made. The session takes about 30 to 35 minutes.
You&#39;ll do three short shopping tasks, fill in two short questionnaires
and answer a few questions about your experience. There are no right or
wrong answers. We&#39;re testing the shop, not you. Taking part is
voluntary, and you can stop at any time without giving a reason.</p>
<p>Before we start, I&#39;ll explain one thing about how we&#39;d like you to
work.&quot;</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="2-gdpr-check-and-recording-2-min">2. GDPR check and recording (2
min)</h3>
<p><strong>Check the information sent beforehand.</strong> Ask:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>&quot;Did you fill in the GDPR form with the information and the consent,
and did you have time to read the information? Do you have any
questions?&quot;</p>
</blockquote>
<p>If not, share the <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeok2nbN-E6R2zKpRNv0_kGEClY4ZbMz7Vtx2fFvwpjUFEtdQ/viewform?usp=dialog">GDPR
form link</a> in the chat and give the participant a moment to read and
fill it in now. No written consent, no test.</p>
<p><strong>Ask before recording:</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p>&quot;We&#39;d like to record your screen and your voice for the whole
session, not your face. Are you okay with that?&quot;</p>
</blockquote>
<p><strong>Start the recording.</strong> Only after a yes: <em>&quot;I&#39;m
starting the recording now. Only your screen and your voice are
recorded, not your face.&quot;</em> The interviewer starts the recording. If
the participant says no or is unsure, there is no recording and no
test.</p>
<h3 id="3-think-aloud-explanation-2-min">3. Think-aloud explanation (2
min)</h3>
<blockquote>
<p>&quot;While you do the tasks, we&#39;d like you to think out loud. That means
saying whatever goes through your mind as you go: what you&#39;re looking
for, what you notice, why you click something, and what you find
confusing or easy. Just talk as if you were alone, and don&#39;t worry about
explaining yourself or sounding smart. If you go quiet, I may remind you
to keep talking. I won&#39;t answer questions or help during the tasks, but
that&#39;s only so everyone gets the same experience.&quot;</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Optional: a 30-second warm-up (<em>&quot;Try it now: describe what you see
on this screen.&quot;</em>).</p>
<h3 id="4-scenario-and-the-three-tasks-about-11-min">4. Scenario and the
three tasks (about 11 min)</h3>
<p>Read in a natural, relaxed tone. <strong>Don&#39;t mention
sustainability.</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p>&quot;Imagine you&#39;ve decided to get a bit more organised with your
everyday routine, and you&#39;re taking lunch and drinks with you during the
week. You open the online shop to pick up a few things you&#39;d really use.
You&#39;ll look for three things, one at a time, and I&#39;ll tell you what.
Choose whatever you&#39;d really pick if you were buying it for
yourself.&quot;</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Send the prototype link of the participant&#39;s condition in the Teams
chat. Read the tasks aloud one at a time, always in this order:</p>
<ol type="1">
<li>Buy a water bottle.</li>
<li>Buy a lunch box to take to work, school or out.</li>
<li>Buy a food container to store leftover food in the fridge or
freezer.</li>
</ol>
<p>[decide how the participant signals that a task is done, for example
by saying &quot;I&#39;ve bought it&quot;]</p>
<p>The interviewer stays silent, observes and takes notes: what is
typed, which suggestion is clicked and its position, which product is
bought. If the participant is quiet for a while: <em>&quot;Please keep
talking.&quot;</em> Do not help and do not answer questions about the tasks.
The observer notes anything to ask about in step 7.</p>
<p>After the last task, say: <em>&quot;Thank you, that was the shopping part.
Now we have a short questionnaire.&quot;</em></p>
<h3 id="5-ues-sf-questionnaire-2-min">5. UES-SF questionnaire (2
min)</h3>
<blockquote>
<p>&quot;Please go back to the email and open link 1. The first question asks
for your participant ID. Fill it in now, and tell me when you&#39;re
done.&quot;</p>
</blockquote>
<p>If the participant can&#39;t find the email, share <a href="https://forms.gle/wfFPgBoJKJLGPKWj8">link 1</a> in the chat. The
interviewer stays quiet. The items are in the UES-SF section below.</p>
<h3 id="6-interview-4-min">6. Interview (4 min)</h3>
<p>The audio is already being recorded. Ask the three open questions,
then let the participant talk. Use silence.</p>
<ol type="1">
<li>&quot;How did you find searching in this shop?&quot;</li>
<li>&quot;How did you decide what to pick?&quot;</li>
<li>&quot;Was there anything that stood out to you?&quot;</li>
</ol>
<p>Probing short or vague answers (&quot;it was fine&quot;, &quot;easy&quot;). Use one of
these:</p>
<ul>
<li>&quot;Can you say more about that?&quot;</li>
<li>&quot;What made it feel [easy / fine / confusing]?&quot;</li>
<li>&quot;Can you give me an example from one of the tasks?&quot;</li>
<li>&quot;What were you thinking at that moment?&quot;</li>
</ul>
<p>Don&#39;t suggest answers and don&#39;t mention sustainability yet.</p>
<h3 id="7-observer-questions-2-min">7. Observer questions (2 min)</h3>
<blockquote>
<p>&quot;[Name], is there anything you&#39;d like to ask?&quot;</p>
</blockquote>
<p>The observer asks extra questions based on what they noticed during
the tasks (a hesitation, a repeated search, an unexpected choice). Keep
them neutral and open, and don&#39;t reveal the purpose of the study.</p>
<h3 id="8-emcb-questionnaire-2-min">8. EMCB questionnaire (2 min)</h3>
<blockquote>
<p>&quot;Thank you. One more short questionnaire. Please go back to the email
and open link 2. Again, the first question asks for your participant ID.
Tell me when you&#39;re done.&quot;</p>
</blockquote>
<p>The items are in the EMCB section below. If the participant can&#39;t
find the email, share <a href="https://forms.gle/AoGbdH4jq9maggZE7">link
2</a> in the chat.</p>
<h3 id="9-debrief-and-renewed-consent-4-min">9. Debrief and renewed
consent (4 min)</h3>
<p>Read the debriefing:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>&quot;Thank you very much. We would now like to tell you more about the
study. We did not tell you everything at the start, because it could
have influenced how you shopped. The study is about sustainable
shopping. Some participants saw search suggestions for sustainable
products, such as stainless steel or plastic-free products, mixed into
the list, placed at the top, or highlighted in green. Others saw the
normal suggestions. We want to find out whether the way suggestions are
shown influences what people click on and choose. Your choices were not
right or wrong, and they will only be analysed together with those of
other participants.&quot;</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Then ask:</p>
<ol type="1">
<li>Did you notice anything about the search suggestions while you were
shopping?</li>
<li>How do you feel about search suggestions being used to steer your
choices in this way? Do you find it helpful, neutral or disturbing?</li>
<li>Would you accept this kind of suggestion in a real online shop? Why
or why not?</li>
<li>Do you have any questions about the study?</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Renewed consent</strong>, a spoken yes on the recording:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>&quot;Now that you know the purpose, do you still agree that your data,
including this recording, may be used as described in the information we
sent you? Please say yes or no out loud.&quot;</p>
</blockquote>
<p>If the participant withdraws, delete the recording and all their
other data the same day. Otherwise: <em>&quot;That&#39;s the end of the session.
I&#39;m stopping the recording now. Thank you very much.&quot;</em></p>
<h2 id="gdpr-form-sent-before-the-session">GDPR form (sent before the
session)</h2>
<p>Form: <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeok2nbN-E6R2zKpRNv0_kGEClY4ZbMz7Vtx2fFvwpjUFEtdQ/viewform?usp=dialog">GDPR
consent form</a></p>
<p>The participant fills this in by email link before the session. It
has two pages, &quot;About you&quot; and &quot;Information about the study&quot;. The text
below follows the form.</p>
<h3 id="page-1-about-you">Page 1: About you</h3>
<ol type="1">
<li>Participant ID (required). Helper text: &quot;You can find your
participant ID (e.g. PH1001) in the email with your Teams link&quot;.</li>
<li>What is your gender? (required): Woman / Man / Non-binary /
other.</li>
<li>What is your age group? (required): 18-29 / 30-44 / 45 or
older.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="page-2-information-about-the-study">Page 2: Information about
the study</h3>
<p><strong>What is the study about?</strong> We are a group of students
in Information Science at Aalborg University Copenhagen. For our student
project, we are testing the search function of an online shop prototype
that we built ourselves. It is modelled on a typical online grocery shop
but is not connected to any real shop, and no real purchases are made.
Some details of the study will only be explained at the end of the
session, because knowing them in advance could influence how you
shop.</p>
<p><strong>What happens during the session?</strong> The session takes
place on Microsoft Teams and lasts about 30–40 minutes. You will
complete three short shopping tasks while thinking aloud, fill in two
short questionnaires and answer a few questions about your
experience.</p>
<p><strong>What is recorded?</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>In Teams:</strong> We record your screen and your voice
during the whole session. Your face is not recorded, and you can keep
your camera off.</li>
<li><strong>On the prototype website:</strong> We use Microsoft Clarity,
a tool that records how you interact with the website: what you click,
how you move the mouse, how you scroll and what you type in the search
bar. Clarity uses cookies for this and stores the data on Microsoft&#39;s
servers, which may be located outside the EU. Clarity only records your
[rest of this sentence is cut off in the screenshots: copy it from the
form]</li>
<li><strong>Questionnaires:</strong> We store your answers to this form
and to the two questionnaires (Google Forms).</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>How is your data protected?</strong> Your data are only used
for our student project and are linked to your participant ID, not your
name. We do not enter your name in Clarity or in the questionnaires. The
data are stored securely, are only accessible to the four group members
and are not shared with anyone other than the services named above.
Quotes in our report are anonymised, so you cannot be recognised. All
data that could identify you, including the recordings in Teams and
Clarity, are deleted by 30 November 2026 at the latest.</p>
<p><strong>Who is responsible?</strong> The four students named below
are responsible for processing your data as private individuals. Aalborg
University is not responsible for this data processing.</p>
<p><strong>Your rights.</strong> Taking part is voluntary. You can stop
at any time without giving a reason. Until 30 January 2027, you can ask
to see, correct or delete your data and withdraw your consent by
contacting us. If you are unhappy with how your data are handled, you
can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).</p>

<p><strong>Consent (required, one box):</strong> I have read and
understood the information above. I am 18 years or older, I agree to
take part in the study, and I agree that my screen and voice are
recorded during the whole session and that my interactions with the
prototype website are recorded with Microsoft Clarity. I know that I can
stop at any time.</p>
<p><strong>May we use anonymised quotes from what you say during the
session in our report?</strong> Yes / No.</p>
<p>Without the consent box, the test does not start. A no to the quotes

<h2 id="ues-sf-questionnaire">UES-SF questionnaire</h2>
<p>Form: <a href="https://forms.gle/wfFPgBoJKJLGPKWj8">UES-SF Google
Form</a></p>
<p>Google Form, opened after the tasks. The participant types the
participant ID and rates 12 statements from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5
(strongly agree). Say: <em>&quot;Please think about the search you just used,
the search bar and its suggestions.&quot;</em> Participants open it only when
asked.</p>
<ol type="1">
<li>I lost myself in this experience.</li>
<li>This search was attractive.</li>
<li>I was absorbed in this experience.</li>
<li>I felt frustrated while using this search.</li>
<li>My experience was rewarding.</li>
<li>I found this search confusing to use.</li>
<li>This search was aesthetically appealing.</li>
<li>I felt interested in this experience.</li>
<li>Using this search was worthwhile.</li>
<li>This search appealed to my senses.</li>
<li>Using this search was taxing.</li>
<li>The time I spent using this search just slipped away.</li>
</ol>
<p>The items are in a fixed, shuffled order. Statements 4, 6 and 11 are
scored in reverse (6 minus the answer). The scale is the User Engagement
Scale Short Form (O&#39;Brien, Cairns, &amp; Hall, 2018), adapted to &quot;this
search&quot;.</p>
<h2 id="emcb-questionnaire">EMCB questionnaire</h2>
<p>Form: <a href="https://forms.gle/AoGbdH4jq9maggZE7">EMCB Google
Form</a></p>
<p>Google Form, opened after the interview. The participant types the
participant ID and answers 10 statements about the products they buy in
everyday life. Say: <em>&quot;The next statements are about the products you
buy in everyday life. Choose the answer that describes you
best.&quot;</em></p>
<p>Scale: 1 never true, 2 rarely true, 3 sometimes true, 4 mostly true,
5 always true.</p>
<ol type="1">
<li>When there is a choice, I always choose the product that contributes
to the least amount of environmental damage.</li>
<li>I have switched products for environmental reasons.</li>
<li>If I understand the potential damage to the environment that some
products can cause, I do not purchase those products.</li>
<li>I do not buy household products that harm the environment.</li>
<li>Whenever possible, I buy products packaged in reusable or recyclable
containers.</li>
<li>I make every effort to buy paper products (toilet paper, tissues,
etc.) made from recycled paper.</li>
<li>I will not buy a product if I know that the company that sells it is
socially irresponsible.</li>
<li>I do not buy products from companies that I know use sweatshop
labor, child labor, or other poor working conditions.</li>
<li>I have paid more for environmentally friendly products when there is
a cheaper alternative.</li>
<li>I have paid more for socially responsible products when there is a
cheaper alternative.</li>
</ol>
<p>The score is the sum of the 10 answers (10 to 50), with no
reverse-coded items. The scale is the Ethically Minded Consumer Behavior
scale (Sudbury-Riley &amp; Kohlbacher, 2016).</p>
